03 / The boundaries

Useful, with limitations.

A routing prediction is a starting point.
Context and careful evaluation still matter.

What this demo knows

  • Short English banking queries only.
  • One label from 77 known intents.
  • No unknown-intent rejection.
  • Scores are not calibrated confidence.
  • Closely related intents can be confused.

An observed failure pattern

A broken virtual card
isn’t a request for a new one.

Yet the model often confuses the two. It recognized only 2 of 39 virtual-card troubleshooting queries in the test set. Just 23 training examples remained for that intent after filtering.
